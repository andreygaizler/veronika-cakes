About

Hi, I’m Veronika

Hi! I’m Veronika, I’m 11 years old, and I love making cakes!

This has been my hobby for a long time, and I bake cakes for every family celebration and holiday.

My favorite holiday is a birthday, and I love making and giving cakes to my friends and relatives!

I make cakes from sponge layers soaked in syrup, seasonal berries (cherries, raspberries, strawberries, blueberries), and cream. For birthday décor, I make numbers out of chocolate.

I’m sure you’ll love my cake!

I bake cakes at home with care for every detail and flavor. For me, each cake should be not only beautiful, but truly delicious — soft sponge, rich cream, and neat decoration.

I take orders for birthdays, family celebrations, and cozy occasions when you want something special. We’ll choose the flavor, size, and design — and I’ll bake a cake for your day.

  • Syrup-soaked sponge layers
  • Seasonal berries and cream
  • Chocolate numbers for birthdays
